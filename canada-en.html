Frequently asked questions

My ex is already with someone else — is it still worth it?
It is one of the situations I handle almost every week. The work first loosens the attachment to the third person, then brings the feelings back to your bond. These cases usually need the longest timeframe.
How do I know you are not like the others?
Because I turn work down. When the reading shows a return is not possible, I say so plainly and charge nothing. That is exactly why the first reading is free — judge my honesty before deciding anything.
Will the other person notice anything?
Never. The work is gentle, and the other person returns convinced the decision is entirely their own. Your enquiry stays strictly between us.
